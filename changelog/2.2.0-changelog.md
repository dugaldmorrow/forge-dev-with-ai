# Changelog: 2.2.0

**Date:** 2-Jun-2026, 15:02 AEST  
**Previous version:** 2.1.3  
**Change type:** MINOR

## Summary

Updated the slide title best practices in the presentation skill and rewrote all 45 slide titles in `Presentation.html` to comply with the new guidelines. The previous guideline allowed 8–15 words across up to 2 lines; the new guideline targets 5–8 words on a single line. All titles were also reviewed for clarity, assertion strength, and specificity. The agenda slide ("What we'll cover today", 4 words) is the only deliberate exception, as a conventional short-form agenda label.

## Changes

### Skills

- **`.agents/skills/presentation-best-practices/SKILL.md`**: Replaced the two separate slide title sections ("Use action titles, not topic labels" and "Slide title length") with a single unified "Slide titles" section. New guidance covers: be clear and specific; keep to 5–8 words / one line; use assertion or action language; maintain parallel structure; make titles work as a standalone storyline; additional tips (front-load key word, sentence case, when to number slides). The pitfalls table column headers were also corrected to Title Case and italic markdown removed from the Agenda cell.

### Presentation

- **`Presentation.html`**: Rewrote all 45 slide titles to comply with the updated 5–8 word / one-line guideline. Titles were shortened and sharpened while preserving the assertion-based, specific framing of the originals. Vague titles (slides 14 and 15, which used "These MCP servers…") were given specific, named subjects. The dual-claim title on slide 38 was resolved into a single sharper assertion. All titles now pass the 5–8 word length test (44/45 strictly within range; the agenda slide at 4 words is a deliberate conventional exception).
