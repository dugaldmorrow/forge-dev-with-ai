## Summary

Revised the spoken portion of speaker notes across `Presentation.html` to ensure they read as actual words the presenter would say aloud, rather than internal stage directions or observations about the slide. Notes that previously described what the slide does ("this is the synthesis slide", "acknowledge the scepticism in the room") were rewritten in first/second person spoken register, often in quotation marks to make the spoken/background distinction unambiguous.
