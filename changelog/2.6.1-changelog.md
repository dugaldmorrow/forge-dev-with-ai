# Changelog: 2.6.1

**Date:** 6-Jun-2026, 22:32 AEST  
**Previous version:** 2.6.0  
**Change type:** PATCH

## Summary

Renamed three of the four AI development tool categories throughout `Presentation.html` to better align the category names with their respective autonomy levels. The old names were generic labels that did not imply the degree of AI autonomy; the new names carry clearer semantic signals that reinforce the autonomy spectrum the presentation teaches.

## Changes

### Presentation

- **Presentation.html**: Renamed three categories across all occurrences (slide 6 table, slide 10 title and speaker notes, slide 11 title and speaker notes, slide 12 comment and title, appendix table and speaker notes):
  - `AI-Assisted Coding Tools` → `AI Coding Assistants` ("Assistants" signals the human is driving; the AI assists, not leads)
  - `AI-Centric IDEs` → `AI-Native IDEs` ("Native" implies AI is baked into the architecture rather than being a plugin, without overstating autonomy)
  - `Managed AI Dev Platforms` → `Autonomous Dev Platforms` ("Autonomous" directly signals the ★★★★ fully-agentic autonomy level)
  - `AI Dev Agents` — unchanged (already accurate and well-understood)
