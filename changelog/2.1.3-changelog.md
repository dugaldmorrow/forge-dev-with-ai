## Summary

Reduced on-slide text density across the entire presentation, applying the presentation best-practices skill. The primary patterns removed were: (1) prose label paragraphs immediately before standalone content elements (code blocks, tables, lists), and (2) slides with two or more primary content elements where one could be moved to speaker notes or merged. No content was removed from the presentation — all moved text was placed into speaker notes.
